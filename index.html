01 The approach

Creative businesses don't have an HR problem. They have a people velocity problem.

The talent is the product. So HR can't sit downstream filing paperwork — it has to shape hiring standards, growth paths, engagement and employer brand at the same speed the work moves.

And underneath the empathy, there's discipline: payroll accuracy, compliance and reporting handled with the rigour I picked up in Big Four operations — so people decisions run on clean data, not vibes.
